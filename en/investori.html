02

Why now

EU legislation sets binding deadlines for treatment plants.

  1. 20251 Jan 2025

    Directive (EU) 2024/3019 enters into force

    New urban wastewater rules, including plant energy audits every 4 years.

  2. 202731 Jul 2027

    Transposition into Slovak law

    Obligations start to apply to Slovak plant operators.

  3. 20302030

    Fit for 55: −55 % emissions

    The EU climate target versus 1990. Methane and nitrous oxide from wastewater plants are among the tracked sources.

  4. 20452045

    Energy-neutral plants

    Plants from 10,000 p.e. must cover energy from their own renewables, with interim milestones.

  • Slovak Waste Act No. 79/2015 – recovery takes priority over disposal
  • Phosphorus – phosphate rock is on the EU critical raw materials list
  • EU Circular Economy Action Plan – integrated nutrient management
03

Investment thesis

Four pillars of the investment case.

  1. 01

    Regulation-driven demand

    Directive 2024/3019 imposes audits, reporting and energy neutrality on plants. Sludge treatment is an area where the technology contributes to them directly.

    2045plant energy-neutrality deadline
  2. 02

    Two revenue streams

    Gate fees for accepting sludge, plus sales of the organic-mineral product as fertiliser feedstock.

    2×income from sludge intake and product sales
  3. 03

    Low operating costs

    No steam, heating or chemicals, no moving parts in the reactor, fully automatable.

    0chemicals or reagents
  4. 04

    Fast deployment

    Prefabricated vertical modules with a small footprint that fit within existing plant sites.

    11months from contract to start-up
