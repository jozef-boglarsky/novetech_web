What data we process

Your name, organisation, e-mail address, area of interest and the content of your message. We only process this data if you send it to us yourself via the contact form or by e-mail.

Purpose and legal basis

We use the data to handle your message and to communicate about possible cooperation or investment. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract) and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in replying to messages received).

Retention period

We keep the data for as long as necessary to handle the communication, at most 3 years from the last contact, unless a contractual relationship arises between us.

Recipients

Our e-mail and web hosting providers may process the data as processors. We do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies and external services

The website uses no analytics or advertising cookies and loads no content from external services. Fonts are stored on our own server.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Article 15 GDPR), rectification (Article 16), erasure (Article 17), restriction of processing (Article 18), data portability (Article 20) and the right to object (Article 21). Send your request to info@novetech.eu.

If you believe the processing breaches the law, you may lodge a complaint with the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic, Hraničná 12, 820 07 Bratislava 27, www.dataprotection.gov.sk.

This information is effective from 15 November 2026.
