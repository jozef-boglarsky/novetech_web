NOVETECH / Product

Output product

Humus and phosphorus back to the soil.

The organic-mineral substrate is free-flowing, non-hygroscopic and odourless. It is a raw material for concentrated organic fertilisers and degraded-soil improvers.

01

Product composition

Content in % by mass, scale 0–100 %.

Applications

  • Direct application to soil in liquid form to raise water capacity and fertility
  • After maturing in troughs at 40–50 % moisture, as fertiliser and soil improver
  • Revitalisation of degraded and reclaimed land
31,8 kg

of phosphorus in every tonne of product. Phosphate rock is on the EU critical raw materials list and Europe imports almost all of it. Closing the nutrient loop from sludge reduces dependence on mineral fertilisers.

The stated composition is based on the technology's technical documentation. Before market launch in Slovakia the product will be certified under the applicable rules on fertilisers and on sludge application to soil.
